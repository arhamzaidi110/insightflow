# InsightFlow analysis: Pakistan annual CPI inflation

## Data and method

The 66 annual observations for Pakistan (1960–2025) come from the World Bank World Development Indicators series `FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG`. The CSV is `pakistan_inflation_world_bank.csv` in this folder. I uploaded the CSV to InsightFlow's analysis service and ran its deterministic `sort_top` and yearly `trend` plans on `Inflation_Rate_Percent`.

## Findings

1. The highest annual inflation rate in this dataset was **30.77% in 2023**. The next highest observation was **26.66% in 1974**.
2. Annual inflation eased from **30.77% in 2023** to **12.63% in 2024** and **3.55% in 2025**—a **27.22 percentage-point** or **88.5% relative** decline from 2023 to 2025. The 2025 rate is still positive, so this indicates prices rose more slowly; it does not mean the overall price level fell.

The source series is annual CPI inflation, not a monthly or city-level series. Findings describe this dataset and do not establish causes or predict future inflation.
